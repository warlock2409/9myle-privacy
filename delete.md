# Account Deletion Request

**Last updated:** 31 December 2025  

At **9myle**, we respect your privacy and your right to control your data.  
This page explains how you can request the deletion of your account and associated data.

---

## How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion in one of the following ways:

### Option 1: From the App
1. Open the **9myle** app  
2. Go to **Profile / Settings**
3. Select **Delete Account**
4. Confirm your request

Your account deletion request will be processed within **7 working days**.

---

### Option 2: Email Request
If you are unable to access the app, you can request deletion via email.

📧 **Email:** support@9myle.com  
📌 **Subject:** Account Deletion Request  

Please include:
- Registered email or phone number
- App name: **9myle**

---

## What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed:
- Personal profile information
- Contact details
- Business or gym information
- App usage data linked to your account

---

## Data Retention

Some non-personal or legally required data may be retained for a limited period to:
- Comply with legal obligations
- Prevent fraud or misuse

This data is not used for marketing or analytics after account deletion.

---

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are completed within **7 working days** of confirmation.
